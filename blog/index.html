Lines without homework is karaoke. Casting has already read the script. Your job is to walk in (or hit record) with research, a goal, and a sense of how the scene sits in the story — then stay loose enough to play.

A self-tape is not a mini film set. Casting is watching on a laptop between other tapes. They need to see you, hear you, and believe the scene. Tech is there so the acting can land. That’s the whole job of the kit.

Character secrets:Something only they know.

A lot of actors invent a private, unscripted fact for the person they are playing — something only that character knows. It never has to hit the page. It adds subtext and specificity without rewriting the dialogue.
