Actor guide

20 September 2026 · Raden Films

Actor tax expenses in the UK:What to track through the year.

January is a terrible time to reconstruct every Spotlight renewal and ring-light receipt from memory. If you’re self-employed as an actor, the useful habit is boring: log income and costs as they happen.

This is not tax advice. Rules change, and “wholly and exclusively” for your trade is HMRC’s line — not a blog’s. Use this as a checklist of what working actors usually record, then confirm with an accountant or HMRC guidance for your situation.

Why audition season creates a paper trail

Self-tapes aren’t free. Neither are casting platforms, headshots, travel to the room, or the percentage your agent takes. Those costs sit next to fee income when you file. Miss the receipts and you’re guessing under pressure.

Making Tax Digital is rolling further into self-assessment for many people — digital records matter more, not less. A simple log beats a shoebox in April.

Costs actors commonly track

Often relevant when they’re for the acting business (not everyday life):

  • Agent / manager commission (and VAT on it, where shown)
  • Spotlight, Equity, casting sites (Mandy, Casting Networks, Backstage, etc.)
  • Headshots, showreel / voice demos, editing
  • Self-tape kit: light, backdrop, mic, tripod, reader tools
  • Coaching or classes that maintain skills you already use
  • Travel to auditions, rehearsals, jobs (mileage or fares — keep a method)
  • Public liability / equipment insurance
  • Accountancy and bookkeeping software

Grey areas people get wrong: everyday clothes, general haircuts, “might use it someday” gadgets. Costume for a specific role is a different conversation than jeans you wear to Tesco. When unsure, ask — don’t invent a category.

A habit that survives busy weeks

  1. Photo or save the receipt the day you spend.
  2. Note what it was for in one line (“tripod — self-tape”).
  3. Separate fee income from reimbursements when you can.
  4. Monthly glance: anything missing from Spotlight / Equity / kit?

Pair money with the job when it helps — which audition the travel was for — so your audition log and your books tell the same story.

A few quick ones

Can I claim self-tape equipment?

Often yes when it’s for the business — confirm treatment (expense vs capital allowances) with your accountant. Keep proof of purchase and purpose.

When is the self-assessment deadline?

Online filing is typically 31 January for the previous tax year. Don’t leave the first receipt hunt until then.

Is this enough to do my tax return?

No. It’s a tracking guide. Use HMRC guidance or a qualified adviser for your return.
