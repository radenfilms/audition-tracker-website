How it works

From the first casting email to the next self-tape — each step is a real tab in the app. Prefer a written system first? Start with how to keep an audition log.

  1. 1

    Add the audition

    In Auditions

    • Create it yourself in a few fields
    • Or import from email, PDF, or paste (Premium)
    • Review the draft, then save
    then
  2. 2

    Keep the file current

    On the audition card

    • Set Pending, Penciled, Callback, Booked, or Not Booked
    • Attach headshot, sides, and self-tape
    • Search and sort whenever you need it
    dates go to
  3. 3

    Put it on the calendar

    In Calendar

    • Auditions, callbacks, production dates, deadlines
    • Month and agenda views
    • Optional sync to Apple Calendar
    then
  4. 4

    See what’s next

    On Home

    • Upcoming dates at a glance
    • Pending and booked counts
    • Quick links into the rest of the app
    then
  5. 5

    See how you’re booking

    In Stats and Emails

    • Booking rate, outcomes, formats, roles
    • Filter by year or month, export a report
    • Casting contacts gather in Emails
    and
  6. 6

    Show up ready

    In Settings tools

    • Measurements for fittings
    • Actor tips and mood playlists
    • Optional Audition Room (Premium, beta)

More in the toolkit

The loop above is the core. These sit in Settings when you need them.

Import with Premium

Paste a casting email, import a PDF, or share from Mail — then review the draft before you save.

Financials

Track income and expenses tied to your work, then export reports when tax season hits.

Typecast & prep

Typecast from your audition history, actor tips, measurements on hand, and mood playlists for warm-ups.

Backups

Export and restore your data from Settings. Your auditions stay on the device unless you use a cloud feature.

iPhone and iPad

One purchase, both devices. Built for a phone in the waiting room and an iPad when you’re doing admin.

Audition Room (beta)

Premium early access to a private actor community, unlocked in stages. Sign in with Apple required.
