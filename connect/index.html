How Connect works

Built for the real casting workflow — not another shared spreadsheet.

  1. Import the casting email

    Forward to your private Connect address for sides PDFs every time, or use the Gmail extension / bookmark for a quick pull. Paste works too.

  2. Review the draft

    Connect fills project, role, instructions, upload links, and forms. Add or swap the PDF, then pick the actor from your roster.

  3. Send to the app

    Your actor gets the request in Audition Tracker — accept, decline, self-tape, or mark tape sent. You see the status update.

Questions

Quick answers before you start a trial.

Is Connect the same as the actor app?

No. Connect is the agency web dashboard. Audition Tracker is the actor app on iPhone and iPad. Together they carry the request from casting email to actor.

What’s the best way to import sides PDFs?

Forward the casting email to your private Connect address (shown under Import after you sign in). That’s the reliable path for attachments. The Gmail extension is faster for breakdown text and links.

Do actors pay for Connect?

No. Actors need Audition Tracker. Your agency invite unlocks Connect requests in the app. The agency pays the Connect subscription.

Where do I sign in?

Agents: connect.auditiontracker.co.uk. Actors: download Audition Tracker, then Toolkit → Connect with your invite code.
